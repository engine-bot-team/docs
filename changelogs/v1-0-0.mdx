## What's included

<Update label="October 12, 2026" description="Engine Bot 1.0.0">

### A focused workspace for every task

Engine Bot keeps the conversation, generated work, and next steps together in one focused workspace. You spend less time switching between tools and more time reviewing the result.

### Better results from the first prompt

The experience gives Engine Bot the context it needs before it starts. Add your goal, constraints, and reference files up front to get a more useful first pass.

### Progress you can follow

Long-running work is easier to understand with clear stages and status updates. You can see what Engine Bot is doing now, what it has completed, and where your input is needed.

### A cleaner handoff

When a task is ready, the result is easier to share with your team. The output highlights what changed and gives you a clear next step.

</Update>
